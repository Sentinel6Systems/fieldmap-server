Map builder

Choose the maps this device should hold. It downloads them once; after that they work without the internet.

Enter the PIN

Whoever set up this device gave the map builder a PIN. It keeps other people on the network from changing the maps.

        2 Choose what to build
        
  
        Each line is one layer of the map. The ? buttons explain them.
        
  
          

  
          
    More options
    
          
      
          
        
        
        
          Standard terrain is measured from space, about every 30 metres, and includes the tops of trees and buildings. In the United States there is a sharper survey of the bare ground, about every 10 metres, made mostly with lasers from aircraft. It shows small features such as stream banks and road cuts, but the terrain layers take roughly ten times the space and time. Outside the United States this choice changes nothing. The third choice adds the sharpest survey there is, every 1 metre, for one small area you place on the map: it shows single ditches, tracks and building platforms in the hillshade. It is kept small on purpose, at most 100 × 100 km, because at that detail a whole state would be far too much for this device. The largest size downloads roughly 50 to 150 GB.
          
      
          
      
          
        
        
          
          
          
        
          
        
          
        
          Choose how large the area is, press “Place it on the map”, then click the map where its middle should be. Zoom in first: town names appear to help you find the spot. The area is drawn as an orange square. It must lie inside a United States region you have chosen. Only the hillshade gets this detail, and only where a 1 metre survey has been published; elsewhere the area keeps the sharper 10 metre terrain.
          
      
          
      
          
        
        
        
          Feet gives a line every 40 feet. Metres gives a line every 20 metres.
          
      
          
      
          
        
        
        
          The map can carry every place name in every language it is known in. Keeping one language makes the base map smaller. Names that do not exist in that language are shown in the local one.
          
      
          
      
          
        
        
          
        
          The roads, buildings and place names come from OpenStreetMap, which is updated every day. This device keeps what it downloaded and uses it again for a month, then downloads it anew by itself. Tick this to download it again now, for example after a road has changed. It takes the time of the download (the size is in step 3).
          
      
          
      
          
        
        
          Without this, the map is blank outside your regions. With it, you see countries, large cities and main roads of the whole world when you zoom out. The first time this is a big job: about 96 GB to download, 160 GB of free space while it works, and several hours. After that it is kept and reused.
          
      
          
    
          
  
          

          


          
  
          3 Check the space and time
          
  
          Choose a region and at least one layer to see how much space the build needs.
          
  
          
    
          
    
          
    
          
    
          
      See what the space is for
      
          
        
          PartStaysWorking roomTime
          
        
      
          
      
          The parts are built one after another, so only one part needs its working room at a time. These are estimates on the safe side, made from the size of the regions before anything is downloaded. Flat country needs less than mountains.
          
      
          
    
          
  
          

          


          
  
          4 Build
          
  
          
    
          Good to know before you start
          
    
            
      
          • A new layer replaces the old one. It is not added to it. If this device has Germany and you now build France, it will have France only. To keep both, choose both.
            • 
      
          • Layers you do not tick stay as they are.
            • 
      
          • The old maps keep working while the new ones are made. They are swapped in one go at the very end. If the build stops early, nothing changes.
            • 
      
          • A build can take hours or days and needs the internet the whole time. You can close this page: the build carries on.

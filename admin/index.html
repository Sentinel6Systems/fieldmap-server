Map builder

Choose the maps for the box. The box downloads them one time. Then the maps work without the internet.

Enter the code

The person who set up the box gave the Map builder a code. The code stops other people on the box's network from changing the maps.

        2 Choose what to build
        
  
        Each line is one layer of the map. Press ? to read about a layer.
        
  
          

  
          
    More options
    
          
      
          
        
        
        
          Satellites measured the standard terrain, about every 30 metres. It includes the tops of trees and buildings. In the United States, a sharper survey of the bare ground has a height about every 10 metres. Lasers on aircraft measured most of it. It shows small features such as stream banks and road cuts. But the terrain layers then need about 10 times the space and time. Outside the United States, this choice changes nothing. The third choice adds the sharpest survey, every 1 metre, for one small area that you place on the map. The hillshade then shows single ditches, tracks and building platforms. This area is small on purpose, 100 × 100 km at the most. A full state at this detail is too much for the box. The largest size downloads about 50 to 150 GB.
          
      
          
      
          
        
        
          
          
          
        
          
        
          
        
          Choose the size of the area. Press Place it on the map. Then click the map where you want the middle of the area. Zoom in first. Town names then help you find the spot. The page shows the area as an orange square. The area must be in a United States region that you chose. Only the hillshade gets this detail, and only where a 1 metre survey exists. In other parts, the area keeps the sharper 10 metre terrain.
          
      
          
      
          
        
        
        
          Feet gives a line every 40 feet. Metres gives a line every 20 metres.
          
      
          
      
          
        
        
        
          The map can hold each place name in all its languages. One language makes the base map smaller. A name that does not exist in that language shows in the local language.
          
      
          
      
          
        
        
          
        
          The roads, buildings and place names come from OpenStreetMap. People update OpenStreetMap every day. The box keeps the data that it downloaded and uses it again for a month. After that, the box downloads it again automatically. Tick this option to download the data again now, for example after a road changed. This adds the time of the download. Step 3 shows the size.
          
      
          
      
          
        
        
          Without this option, the map is blank outside your regions. With it, you see the countries, large cities and main roads of the world when you zoom out. The first time, this is a large job. It downloads about 96 GB, needs 160 GB of free space while it works, and takes several hours. After that, the box keeps it and uses it again.
          
      
          
    
          
  
          

          


          
  
          3 Check the space and time
          
  
          Choose a region and at least one layer. This step then shows the space that the build needs.
          
  
          
    
          
    
          
    
          
    
          
      Show what the space is for
      
          
        
          PartStaysWorking roomTime
          
        
      
          
      
          The box builds the parts one after the other. So only one part needs its working room at a time. These numbers are safe estimates from the size of the regions, before the downloads. Flat land needs less than mountains.
          
      
          
    
          
  
          

          


          
  
          4 Build
          
  
          
    
          Read this before you start
          
    
            
      
          • A new layer replaces the old layer. The build does not add to it. If the box has Germany and you build France, the box then has only France. To keep both, choose both.
            • 
      
          • Layers that you do not tick do not change.
            • 
      
          • The old maps work until the build is complete. The box puts all the new maps in place together at the end. If the build stops before the end, nothing changes.
            • 
      
          • A build can take hours or days. It needs the internet all the time. You can close this page. The build continues.

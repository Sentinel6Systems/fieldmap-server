1. About the box

The box is a small computer that keeps maps. It shows the maps to computers, phones and tablets near it. It does not need the internet to do this.

With the box, you can:

  • Look at the maps in a browser.
  • Print paper maps, or make PDF files of the maps.
  • Use the maps in the Fieldmap app on Android phones and tablets.
  • Use the maps in ATAK, WinTAK, iTAK and other map apps.

The box also has two optional functions. Remote access lets devices use the box through the internet. Sharing lets people see where the others are, and share markers. The box does not need them to show maps. See section 8.

In this guide, a device is a phone or a tablet. The box's network is the Wi-Fi or the cable network that the box is on. Section 11 gives the meaning of more words.

2. Open the pages of the box

  1. Connect your computer or device to the box's network.
  2. Open the browser. For example, open Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari.
  3. Type the address of the box in the address bar at the top. Press Enter.

This guide is on the box now. So you can use this address for the other pages too:

the address in the address bar of this browser

If you do not know the address, ask the person who set up the box. Write the address on the box or on a card. Save the page as a bookmark in the browser.

Each page of the box has a header at the top. The header has a tab for each page. Press a tab to open its page. The tab of the page that is open has a line under it.

Press the Maps tab. The map fills the page.

Change the map style

The Map style list is at the top left. Choose a style from the list. The box can have different styles, for example a light map for the day and a dark map for the night. The browser keeps your choice.

Keep a place

The address in the address bar changes when you move the map. The address keeps the place and the zoom. Save it as a bookmark to come back to the same place. When you go to Print a map, that page opens at the same place.

NoteThe map shows only the regions that the box has. Outside them, the map is empty or shows only the large features of the world.

4. Print a map

Press the Print a map tab. The map fills the page. A panel beside the map has the settings. A blue rectangle on the map shows the area of the sheet. To hide the panel, press ▾ at the top of the panel.

Print one sheet

  1. In Map style, choose the style of the map.
  2. In Scale, choose the scale. A large number shows more ground with less detail.
  3. In Paper, choose the size of the paper in your printer.
  4. In Orientation, choose Portrait (tall) or Landscape (wide).
  5. Move the sheet to the place that you want. Drag the blue rectangle. Or tap the map outside the rectangle. The sheet then moves to that point.
  6. Type a title in Title if you want one.
  7. In Quality, choose Standard or Best. Best gives sharper lines but takes more time.
  8. Press Print. The browser opens its print window. Choose the printer and print.

To make a PDF file and not print, press Download PDF. You can print the file later, or send it to another person.

Bring sheet here moves the sheet to the middle of the map on the screen. Fit map to sheet moves the map on the screen to show all of the sheet.

Under the settings, Ground area shows the size of the ground on the sheet. Grid shows the grid on the sheet. Centre shows the position of the middle of the sheet.

Print many sheets (an atlas)

An atlas is a set of sheets that join together, with an index page. In Pages, choose an atlas size, for example Atlas 2 × 2. The blue rectangle then shows all the sheets.

CautionUse a laptop or a desktop computer for an atlas. On a phone or a tablet, the browser can stop. The browser makes every sheet itself, and an atlas needs much memory.

To put the sheets in a 3-ring binder, tick Make the left margin of atlas pages wider.

Show or hide parts of the map

Open Layers. Each switch shows or hides one part of the map, for example contour lines or buildings. The change shows on the screen and on the paper. Show everything shows all parts again.

Add markers, arrows and shapes

  1. Choose a colour under Colour for new items.
  2. Press Add marker, Add arrow, Circle, Rectangle or Polygon.
  3. Read the hint under the buttons. It tells you where to tap. For example, an arrow needs a tap at its start and a tap at its end.
  4. For a polygon, tap each corner. Then press Finish shape.

The items show in the lists Markers, Arrows and Shapes. There you can give each item a name, or remove it. Remove all removes all of them. Units sets feet and miles or metres and kilometres. To show the lengths and the areas of the shapes, tick Show measurements on the map.

To print a list of the items and the map symbols, tick Add a key page.

CautionSave your markers, arrows and shapes to a file if you need them later. Only this browser keeps them. Another browser or another computer does not show them.

To save them, press Save markers, arrows & shapes. To open a saved file, press Load markers, arrows & shapes. The file does not keep the map or the print settings.

Terrain: what you can see, and where a radio can reach

Some boxes have height data. Then the panel has a Terrain section.

  • Viewshed shows what a person can see from a point.
  • Radio shows where a VHF or UHF radio can probably reach from a point. It can also check one link between two points.

Press Place. Then tap the map to put the point. You can drag a point to move it. The colours on the map show the result. You can print them with the map.

CautionDo not trust the result without a check on the ground. The result is an estimate from the shape of the ground. Trees, buildings and weather can make the real result worse.

The magnetic model

The printed map shows the difference between true north and magnetic north. This difference changes slowly each year. When the model on the box is old, the panel shows a message. Press Update magnetic model to download a new model. This works only when the box has a connection to the internet.

5. Use the maps in ATAK and other apps

ATAK, WinTAK, iTAK and many other map apps can show the maps of the box. They need a tile address.

  1. On the Maps page, press Use in another app.
  2. Find the map style that you want. Press Copy beside its address.
  3. In the other app, add a new map source from a tile address. Each app does this in a different way. Look for words such as "map source", "custom map", "XYZ" or "URL" in its settings.
  4. Paste the address.

The address works for devices that connect to the box in the same way as your browser does now.

6. The Fieldmap app

Fieldmap is an app for Android phones and tablets. It shows the maps of the box. It can keep parts of the map on the device, for use away from the box.

Get the app

You get the app from the box. You do not need an app store or an account.

  1. Connect the device to the box's network.
  2. In the browser of the device, open the address of the box.
  3. Press the Admin tab. Then press Devices.
  4. Do the steps under Get the Fieldmap app.

If the Devices page has no app, the person who looks after the box must add it first.

Connect the app to the box

  1. Connect the device to the box's network.
  2. Open the app. Press Menu. Press Box.
  3. Press Find the box. Or type the address of the box and press Connect.

When the app connects, the map shows. To change the map style, press Menu, Map style.

Show your position

Press Follow. The first time, Android asks for permission to use the location. Allow it. The map then shows your position as a blue dot and moves with you. North is at the top (North up). Press the button again to turn the map in the direction that you face (Head up). When you move the map with your finger, the map stops following you.

Your position stays on your device. The app does not send it to the box or to other people. Only the optional sharing function sends it (section 8).

Keep maps on the device (map areas)

A map area is a part of the map that the app keeps on the device. Away from the box's network, the app uses its map areas. Save map areas before you go away from the box.

  1. Connect to the box.
  2. Press Menu, Map areas, Save a map area.
  3. Move and zoom the map. Put the area that you want in the frame.
  4. Type a name. Choose the Detail. A lower number gives a smaller download with less detail.
  5. Read the size and the free space. Then press Download.

When the box gets new maps, press Menu, Map areas, Update all map areas. Keep the app open until the download is complete.

Markers

  • To add a marker, touch and hold the map for 2 seconds.
  • To add a marker at your position, touch and hold the blue dot.
  • To change a marker, tap it. You can give it a name and a colour. You can Move or Delete it.
  • To see all markers, press Menu, Markers.

On the Markers screen, Save to a file saves the markers in a GeoJSON file. Add from a file opens one. You can open the same file on the Print a map page with Load markers, arrows & shapes.

Close the app

To stop the app fully, press Menu, Close Fieldmap.

7. Admin: the Map builder

The Admin tab has pages for the person who looks after the box. Most people do not need them. Some boxes ask for the box's code on these pages. The person who set up the box has the code.

This section tells you about the Map builder. The Remote access page and the sharing switch on the Devices page are for the optional functions in section 8.

Put maps on the box

The Map builder puts maps on the box. The box downloads the map data one time. After that, the maps work without the internet. Some boxes do not have the Map builder. On these boxes, the person who set up the box adds the maps.

  1. Press Admin, Map builder.
  2. Choose your regions. Click a region on the map, for example a state. Click it again to remove it. You can also find a region by its name.
  3. Choose what to build. Each line is one layer of the map. Press ? beside a line to read about it.
  4. Check the space and time. The page shows the space on the drive and the time that the build needs.
  5. Build. Read the notes. Press Start the build. Then press Yes, start the build.
CautionChoose all the regions that you want to keep. A new build replaces the old maps of the layers that you build. If the box has one region and you build a different one, the box then has only the new region.

A build needs the internet all the time. It can take hours or days. You can close the page. The build continues. The old maps work until the build is complete. If the build stops before the end, the maps do not change. To stop a build, press Stop the build.

8. Optional: remote access and sharing

The box and the app do not need these functions. Without them, the box shows maps, prints maps and gives maps to the app. Both functions are off when you get the box. Switch them on only if you need them.

  • Remote access lets paired devices use the box from outside the box's network, through the internet. There are two ways in: a port on the router, or NetBird. The box uses one way at a time.
  • Sharing lets the devices see the positions of the other devices, and share markers.

The two functions are separate. You can use sharing without remote access. Then only the devices on the box's network share.

Switch on remote access

Only paired devices can come in from outside.

  1. Press Admin, Remote access.
  2. Press Switch on. Pairing then opens for 10 minutes.
  3. In Way in, make sure that Port on the router is chosen. This is the usual way.
  4. Read From outside. It tells you if devices can reach the box from outside.

The box asks the router to open a port. Some routers do not do this. Then tick I forward the port on the router myself. The page shows the port and the address to type on the router. Type the internet address of the router in the field below it. Then press Save. The status page of the router shows its internet address.

To stop remote access, press Switch off. The box then closes the port on the router. The paired devices stay on the list.

CautionSwitch off remote access when nobody needs it. Then nothing outside the box's network can use the box.

Some internet connections do not let a router open a port. Then From outside tells you that devices cannot connect to the box. Use NetBird for these connections. See the next part.

The box uses one way in at a time. When you choose NetBird, the box closes the port on the router. When you choose the port on the router, the box stops NetBird. The box also changes its firewall for the way that you choose.

Come in through NetBird

NetBird is a service on the internet that connects computers and devices in a private network. The box and the paired devices become members of your NetBird network, and the devices reach the box through it.

Use NetBird when the router cannot open a port. This occurs with:

  • Starlink
  • internet from the mobile network
  • a router that you cannot change, for example in a rented building or on a camp site

NetBird is only the way to the box. The box does the same check of each device as before. A member of your NetBird network that is not a paired device gets nothing from the box.

You need a NetBird account. NetBird has a free plan for small networks. Only you need the account. The persons who use the devices do not need an account, and they type nothing.

CautionMake a NetBird account that is only for the box. Do not use a NetBird network that has other computers in it. Each paired device becomes a member of the network. A member can reach the other computers in the network.

NetBird step 1: make an account

  1. Use a computer that has an internet connection.
  2. Open app.netbird.io in the browser.
  3. Sign up for a new account. The page tells you how.
  4. If NetBird asks you to add a computer or to install NetBird, close that window. You do not install NetBird on your computer.

The pages of your NetBird account are the NetBird dashboard.

NetBird step 2: make a setup key

A setup key lets the box and the paired devices join your NetBird network.

  1. In the NetBird dashboard, press Settings. It is at the bottom of the menu on the left.
  2. Press Setup Keys.
  3. Press Create Key. Some versions of the dashboard show Create Setup Key.
  4. In Name, type Fieldmap.
  5. Switch on Make this key reusable.
  6. Do not type a number in Usage limit.
  7. Remove the number in Expires in. The field then shows Unlimited, and the key does not expire.
  8. Do not switch on Ephemeral Peers. Do not change the other fields.
  9. Press Create Setup Key.
  10. NetBird shows the setup key one time. Copy the setup key now.
  11. Press Close.
CautionKeep the setup key secret. A person who has the setup key can add a computer to your NetBird network.
NoteIf you keep a number of days in Expires in, the key stops after that time. The box and the devices that joined before continue to work. A device that you pair after that time cannot join. Then make a new setup key and type it on the box.

NetBird step 3: connect the box

  1. On the box's network, press Admin, Remote access.
  2. Make sure that remote access is on.
  3. In Way in, choose NetBird. The part NetBird then shows below.
  4. In Setup key, type the setup key. You can also paste it.
  5. Do not change Management server.
  6. Press Connect. The page asks you to agree. Press OK.
  7. Wait. The first time, the box downloads NetBird. This can take some minutes.

The box then closes the port on the router. Devices outside can come in only through NetBird.

When it works, the page shows The box is connected to the NetBird network. The box keeps the setup key. The page does not show the setup key again.

If the page shows The NetBird network did not accept the setup key, make a new setup key. Then type it and press Connect. Until NetBird connects, no device can come in from outside.

NetBird step 4: the devices

The devices get the setup key from the box. The box gives it only to paired devices.

  • A device that you pair now gets the setup key when you pair it. See Pair a device.
  • A device that you paired before gets the setup key automatically. Connect the device to the box's network and open the app one time.
CautionChange the way in only when the devices are on the box's network, or can come to it. A device that is outside when you change the way cannot connect until it is on the box's network one time.

Outside the box's network, the app uses NetBird automatically, and it shows Connected.

Make sure that NetBird works

  1. Take a paired device off the box's network. For example, switch off Wi-Fi on a phone that has mobile data.
  2. Open the app. Wait one minute.
  3. Make sure that the app shows Connected and that the map moves.
  4. On the box's network, open Admin, Remote access. Read From outside.

From outside then shows A device connected to the box through NetBird, with the date. In the NetBird dashboard, Peers shows the box as fieldmap-box. Each device shows as Fieldmap and the name of the device.

Stop NetBird, or remove a lost device

To stop NetBird, choose Port on the router in Way in. The box then stops NetBird and opens the port on the router again. The devices stop their use of NetBird. The box keeps the setup key. To use NetBird again, choose NetBird.

If a device is lost, do these steps:

  1. On the Devices page, press Remove beside the device. The device then gets nothing from the box.
  2. In the NetBird dashboard, press Peers. Delete the device there.
  3. In Settings, Setup Keys, revoke the setup key. The lost device has a copy of it.
  4. Make a new setup key.
  5. On the Remote access page, press Change the setup key. Type the new setup key. Press Connect.

The other devices continue to work. They do not need the new setup key.

The box is a member of the NetBird network already, so NetBird does not check the new setup key. The page tells you this. In the NetBird dashboard, press Settings, Setup Keys. Make sure that the new key shows as valid.

Advanced: your own NetBird server

You can run a NetBird server yourself. Then you do not use NetBird's service. This is work for a person who knows servers. The NetBird documentation at docs.netbird.io tells you how.

  1. Make sure that the box and the devices can reach your server from the internet.
  2. Make a setup key in the dashboard of your server, as in step 2.
  3. On the Remote access page, type the address of your server in Management server, for example https://netbird.example.com.
  4. Type the setup key and press Connect.

Pair a device

A paired device can use the box from outside. Pair each device one time, on the box's network.

  1. Make sure that remote access is on.
  2. Connect the device to the box's network. Open the Fieldmap app on it.
  3. On a computer or on the device, open Admin, Devices.
  4. Press Pair a device.
  5. In the app, press Menu, Box, Pair this device. Do this in 10 minutes or less.

The device then shows under Paired devices. To stop a device from outside, press Remove beside it. Do this at once if a device is lost.

A paired device uses the box through the internet when it is not on the box's network. The app does this automatically.

Switch on sharing

Sharing has two switches. The person who looks after the box switches on sharing on the box first. Then each person switches on sharing in the app.

  1. Press Admin, Devices.
  2. In Sharing positions and markers, press Switch on.

The card then lists the devices that share now, and the number of shared markers. Press Switch off to stop sharing for all devices.

CautionDo not switch on sharing on a network that strangers can join. All devices on the box's network can then see the shared positions.

Share your position

  1. Connect the app to the box.
  2. Press Menu, Sharing.
  3. Type your name in Your name on the map. The others see this name.
  4. Press Share my position.

Sharing continues when you leave the app or when the screen goes off. To stop, press Stop sharing my position. Or press Menu, Close Fieldmap.

The Sharing screen lists the other devices that share. Tap a name to see the position and when it came. A position that is old shows a different look. The person can be at a different place now.

Share a marker

  1. Tap the marker.
  2. Tick Share with the other devices.

All devices on the box then show the marker. All of them can change it or delete it. Untick to keep the marker on your device only.

NoteThe box does not keep the positions. A device that stops sharing goes off the map of the others in about 2 minutes. The box keeps the shared markers.

9. Take care of the box

  • Keep the box on when people use it. The devices cannot get maps from a box that is off.
  • Keep the box in a dry, cool place with air around it.
  • Do not remove the power during a build. The build stops and you must start it again.
  • Keep the address of the box and the box's code on paper, in a safe place.
  • Keep a paper copy of this guide with the box. Press Print this guide at the top of this page.
NoteThe box keeps its maps when it is off. After you switch it on again, wait some minutes. The pages then open again.
